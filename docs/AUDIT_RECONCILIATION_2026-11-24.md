## Concrete defects closed in the integration source

- A bare exact reviewed place answer could be rejected by Search v2 instead of
  narrowing the prior search. The new path retains person/date/media/text and
  still revalidates current access and source.
- Month-only corrections in Search v2/v3 could switch to the current year. They
  now use the active search's unique year; explicit years take precedence and
  ambiguous multi-year ranges still clarify.
- A blank story suggestion could overwrite authored text in a chapter without
  selected sources. The editor preserves unchanged wording and applies AI
  attribution only to a real wording change. Review, explicit save and identical
  retry after an uncertain save remain separate actions.
- One failed Jude capability read could leave the composer unavailable until a
  page reload. An explicit reopen can retry a transient failure through the
  existing scoped restore gate, without creating a turn, conversation or capture.
- The standard source archive omitted the roadmap module and assets. Fixed
  immutable Git-source mappings and supported source/bundle root resolution now
  close that dependency. Extracted smoke requests both language pages and all
  five actual asset bytes, with checkout fallback denied.

These fixes were subsequently [delivered on Windows](RELEASE_2026-11-24_FUNCTIONAL_FIXES.md)
from merged main `c7a28ef`, retaining the original base runtime and qualified
native kernels. The first transaction rolled back after a pre-worker scheduler
snapshot timeout; the corrected no-console launch passed, followed by actual
HTTPS and natural SYSTEM grouping handoff checks and explicit owned release.
Grouping deferred work with zero updates, so this is not a full index sweep or
real-model relevance result. Overlapping local cohorts must not be summed into
a larger claimed test total. Optional TV approval remains uninstalled here.

## Where the audit needs qualification

The proposed search, voice and relevance targets require an agreed dataset,
hardware, denominators and representative samples. They do not silently replace
frozen historical contracts, and subjective prose or aspirational latency must
not again block unrelated functional delivery. Access, source integrity,
cancellation, resource bounds and explicit editorial actions remain hard limits.

The current Rust installation/binding evidence is narrower than full household
quality, but stronger than a blanket statement that all Rust work is merely an
uninstalled proposal. Likewise, generated browser or native-component tests do
not establish usability on a family's phone, TV, microphone or library.

No general superiority over PhotoPrism is claimed. Competitive specifications
should be refreshed from primary documentation when used for a concrete product
comparison. A family knowledge graph, event understanding, iOS backup and a
permission-aware external interface remain staged opportunities, rather than
reasons to replace the current backend or database.

## Household walkthrough

Have a family member try each task through the interface they normally use.
Record the browser or installed app version, the library label, elapsed time,
whether they completed the task on their own, and where they needed help. Use
generated media for an import or a new story in an evaluation library. Keep
any private feedback with the household's acceptance record.

| Task | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Import and review | Find where a photo and short video went. Read the receipt, distinguish preparation from a playback failure, and retry when ready. |
| Find a person or event in Jude | Find a reviewed person by name, then narrow the same conversation by date and media type. Confirm the displayed filters and open a matching result. |
| Inspect media | Open a photo fullscreen, pinch to see detail, go to the next photo, and exit. Open a prepared video and use its fullscreen player. |
| Arrange and reopen a story | Select frames in the intended order, compare the family wording with any proposed edits, explicitly save, then reopen and check the order and wording. |
| Browse on TV | Open authorized media through the intended installed TV mode. In guest mode, verify that private story review remains unavailable. |
| Pause or recover | Pause voice, resume the conversation, and recover a completed request without sending it again. Verify that leaving the panel stops microphone capture and reply playback. |

The generated checks cover parts of these tasks. Record the physical-device
outcomes separately, including keyboard or enlarged text use where relevant.
